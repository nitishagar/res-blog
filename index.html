01 — About

An engineer who ended up leading engineers.

I started in distributed systems and performance work — the kind where a 3,000ms search becomes a 200ms one and conversion jumps 15%. These days I lead GoDaddy engineering orgs behind performance marketing at scale: 120+ engineers across automated testing — delivering $80M+ in value — and a recommendations system serving 20M+ customers, with multi-agent QA running around the clock.
